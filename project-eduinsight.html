EduInsight — Student Analytics

An interactive student performance analytics dashboard designed to visualize academic performance, attendance, course scores, and identify students who may need academic support.

About The Project

EduInsight is a student performance analytics dashboard developed to provide a clear overview of academic data. The dashboard allows users to monitor student performance, analyze GPA and attendance, compare course scores, and identify students who may require additional academic support.
